About

I spend most nights in the cold, waiting for the air to settle.

A single image often holds 20 to 60 hours of exposure, gathered over weeks and calibrated by hand.

I travel to the darkest skies I can reach (the Atacama, Namibia, La Palma) and run a small remote observatory in New Mexico for everything else.
