A community for database people

For engineers who read the query plan.

Write-Ahead is where software engineers talk storage engines, indexes, replication and the incidents that taught them. One paper a month, member talks, and a chat that stays on topic.

Free. One email a month. No recruiters, no sponsors.

What we do

three formats, all online

  • monthly

    Paper reading group

    One paper or book chapter a month, from the classics to recent systems research. A member leads, everyone else brings questions. Skimming counts.

  • every 6 weeks

    Member talks

    Twenty minutes on something you built, tuned or broke: a zero-downtime migration, a custom index, a post-mortem you’re finally allowed to share.

  • always on

    The chat

    For the questions you can’t ask at work. Why is this query slow, is this schema a mistake, which isolation level do I actually need.
